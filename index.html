Play instruments with your hands

No pads, no taps. Hold up fingers to pick a note or chord, and shape it by moving your hands — pick an instrument up top once you're in.

FingersRight hand finger count picks the note/chord. Fist = silence (or replay, for drums/piano/guitar/bass).
HeightRight hand up/down controls volume — up is louder.
Left handOpen vs closed controls brightness (tone), always — same rule for every instrument.
SpreadFor Chords: move hands apart to widen it.
Tip: good, even light on your hands matters most — face a window or lamp rather than sitting backlit or in the dark.
